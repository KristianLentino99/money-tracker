# Product Context

## Product

Money Tracker is an operational financial-management web app for individuals.
The current primary platform is the web; a PWA experience is a future
direction.

## Purpose

Help people maintain a clear, reliable view of their personal finances and
plan income, expenses, debt, and savings with confidence.

## Positioning

The product provides complete financial control while preserving awareness of
spending. It intentionally does not use bank connections as a feature because
automatic synchronization can make users lose touch with what they spend.

## Users

Individual users managing their own personal finances.

## Product UI Register

- Clear, simple, and pragmatic.
- Short explanations should accompany configuration and difficult workflows.
- The interface should stay beside the user when decisions or setup require
  attention.
- Avoid overly formal or complex language.
- Avoid overloaded screens.
- Explain automations clearly before and while they act.

## Accessibility

Follow accessibility best practices from the start: sufficient contrast,
visible focus states, keyboard support, semantic structure, and responsive
behavior across desktop, mobile, and the future PWA experience.
